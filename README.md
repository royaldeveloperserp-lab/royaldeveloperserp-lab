🔐 Engineering Principles

Security First

Authentication, authorization, RBAC, and database-level security are treated as core architecture.

AI-Native

AI should be integrated into business workflows rather than existing only as a chatbot.

Modular

Business systems should be modular, maintainable, and extensible.

Business-First

Technology should solve real business problems.

Automation

Repetitive operational workflows should increasingly become intelligent and automated.

Human + AI

AI should provide better information, better tools, and better automation while keeping people involved in important decisions.
